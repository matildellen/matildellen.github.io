## Community involvement

<div class="research-box">

<p>
<strong>GeWoNN – German Word Nerd Network</strong><br>
GeWoNN brings together researchers working on language and reading research in German.
The network promotes open discussion of research practices and aims to improve
methodological standards in the field.
</p>

<p>
<strong>TRUST Network</strong><br>
The TRUST network focuses on improving transparency and reproducibility in reading
research by developing community guidelines and shared best practices.
</p>

<p>
<strong>WiCS Europe+</strong><br>
I serve on the leadership board of Women in Cognitive Science Europe+, an initiative
that supports networking, mentoring, and visibility for women and underrepresented
researchers in cognitive science.
</p>

</div>
